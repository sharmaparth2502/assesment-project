Three-Tier Architecture

The CABO Verification & Settlement Pipeline

From sunlight on a residential rooftop to certified enterprise carbon offsets.

01. Distributed Generation

Rooftop solar producers install smart bi-directional IoT gateway meters that log high-frequency kilowatt generation directly at the inverter level.

02. Tamper-Proof Telemetry

Data packets are cryptographically verified and audited every 15 minutes. Grid baseline emission factors determine exact kilograms of avoided CO₂.

03. Institutional Clearing

Verified batches are tokenized into tradeable Carbon Credits (CC). Corporate buyers retire credits, while 96% of proceeds are transferred directly to producers.
